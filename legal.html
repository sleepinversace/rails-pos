Using Rails POS

  • While your subscription (or trial) is active you may use Rails POS in your shop, on as many devices as you pay for. You may not copy, resell or rent out the software, or try to break its security.
  • You are responsible for your shop's logins: the shop account email and password, and your staff's names and PINs. Keep them private and remove staff who leave.
  • You are responsible for what you sell and for your tax (VAT, income tax and keeping records). Rails POS prints tax invoices, credit notes and VAT totals from what you enter, but please have your accountant check that your settings (VAT number, VAT rate, wording) are right for your business.
  • Card payments are taken on your own card machine; Rails POS records them but does not process card payments or hold any money.

Availability and backups

  • Rails POS keeps working on each device without internet, and backs up to the cloud when there is a connection. We work hard to keep it running, but we can't promise it will never be unavailable (for example, a cloud or internet outage, a lost or broken device, or a browser problem).
  • Data saved only on a device (before it synced, or with cloud sync off) can be lost if that device is lost or its browser data is cleared. Keep cloud sync on and use Settings → Email me my data now and then for your own copy.
  • We may update the app to add features and fix problems. Updates install when you tap "Reload now".

3. Privacy policy (POPIA)

This explains how we handle personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we keep, and why

InformationWhy

  • We never sell personal information, and we don't use your shop's or customers' data for advertising.
  • Receipts, statements and reminders sent by WhatsApp or email go from your device and your own WhatsApp or email app.
  • The app stores your shop data on your device (in the browser) so it works offline. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • Cloud backups and sync use Supabase, a database service with servers in the European Union. Personal information is therefore stored outside South Africa, in a region with data-protection law (GDPR) at least as strong as POPIA, which POPIA allows.
  • Information is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Each shop can only reach its own records (enforced by the database), staff PINs are stored as one-way scrambled codes, and only the Rails POS owner can use the supplier admin page, with every change logged.
  • If we become aware that your information was accessed by someone without permission, we will tell you as soon as reasonably possible, as POPIA requires.

How long we keep it

  • While your subscription is active. After you cancel, we keep your shop's data for 90 days (so you can come back or export it), then delete it, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or the law requires us to keep something (for example our own billing records).

Your rights

  • You may ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how it is used. Contact us on WhatsApp (section 6). You can also export your shop data yourself at any time (Settings → Email me my data).
  • If you are unhappy with how we handle personal information, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.

4. Your customers' information (you are in charge)

For the details of your customers, your shop is the responsible party under POPIA and Rails POS is your operator (we process it only for you). Under section 21 of POPIA, we agree that we will:

  1. Use your customers' information only to provide Rails POS to you, and not for any other purpose.
  2. Keep it confidential and protected with the security measures described in section 3.
  3. Tell you straight away if we believe it has been accessed or acquired by someone without permission.
  4. Delete it when you cancel (after the 90 days in section 3), or sooner if you ask.

You agree to collect only the customer details you need (for example for lay-bys or receipts), to tell your customers why you keep them (see the notice below), and to remove a customer's details if they ask.
